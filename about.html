About 415chat

415chat is San Francisco, talking. A place room for the Bay, not a storefront, not a news desk, not X.

People post about the city: fog and microclimates, Muni and BART when you've actually got a delay, neighborhoods, food fights, the bridge on a clear morning. Guest can browse. Sign-in is for a handle and posts. Direct messages are not live yet.

Room Brief on the right is the official NWS forecast for the SF Bay. It is not a news ingest. SubX does not invent transit delays, crime stats, or events.

FACT only. This brief is the National Weather Service forecast for the SF Bay (NWS grid MTR). Not a news feed.

This site is a public beta. You can read without an account. Posting in the room requires an account, and every account holder must be 18 or older. Public pages (except /report.html) are open to search indexing.
